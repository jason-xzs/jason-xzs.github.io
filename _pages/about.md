I am a master's student in Control Science and Engineering at Beijing Institute of Technology, expected to graduate in June 2027. My work centers on embodied intelligence for mobile robots, especially world-model-based policy learning, spatial prediction, and planning for navigation and manipulation.

Since October 2025, I have been a research assistant in Prof. Hao Tang's lab at the School of Computer Science, Peking University. I am currently working on mobile world models for embodied navigation, including action-conditioned rollout consistency, diffusion-based training, inference acceleration, and deployment on mobile robot platforms.

## Research Interests

- World models for embodied navigation and robot manipulation
- 3D spatial intelligence and embodied perception
- Trajectory planning and optimization for mobile and multi-robot systems
- Physically grounded policy learning and executable motion generation

## Selected Work

### MWM: Mobile World Models for Action-Conditioned Consistent Prediction

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07799) / [Project Page](https://aigeeksgroup.github.io/MWM) / [Code](https://github.com/AIGeeksGroup/MWM)

MWM studies action-conditioned mobile world models for consistent multi-step prediction and planning-based image-goal navigation. My work in this project focuses on world-model training and deployment, including diffusion-based two-stage training, action-conditioned consistency, inference acceleration, and robot-side testing.
